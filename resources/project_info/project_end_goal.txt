The end gols of the project is to evaluate various LLM's answering abilities. that's why we start with a relatively strict policy of question generation and rating, to make sure we have a wide span of possible questions to have a model answering. Then we want to evaluate those answers. The user case here is a person working in a spefcific field, needing to eventually feed the model a doc or few and get quick and reliable answers to pressing matters, wether they require hunting for specific details in a sea of data, performing a deeper analysis, or using the big picture rising form the doc to make cpmarsions, form strategies and predictions, etc.
